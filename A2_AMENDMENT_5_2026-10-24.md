# A2 — Amendment 5 · 2026-10-24, camera-ready (post-hoc penalty analysis)

This amendment adds to `A2_PREDECLARATION_2026-09-03.md` and records what the camera-ready version adds after acceptance. Every pre-registered verdict and every stamped value is unchanged. All results below are **post hoc**.

## Why a post-hoc analysis

All four S4 arms minimise a per-row negative log-likelihood with the noise scale σ estimated jointly, plus 0.5 λ‖w‖² with λ = 1e-3.

- λ was fixed in code before pre-registration. The pre-registration text does not name it; Amendment 4 records it as fixed a priori.
- On a squared-error scale this is a ridge penalty of about n λ σ̂². That is far below what cross-validation selects.
- Where the exact-only training set is close to the 1024 fingerprint bits, the exact-only fit nearly interpolates. Those targets carry most of the naive advantage (naive − exact-only):

| exact training rows | targets | naive − exact-only |
|---|---|---|
| < 1024 | 15 | −0.122 |
| 1024–1500 | 42 | −0.179 |
| ≥ 1500 | 101 | −0.029 |

## What was run

The script and its outputs are in `penalty_sensitivity/`.

1. **Regression gate.** The original fit was re-run and reproduces every published per-seed RMSE exactly.
2. **Corrected optimiser.** The log σ bound is now an L-BFGS-B constraint, and the censored term uses `log_ndtr`. At λ = 1e-3 this changes no conclusion.
3. **Fixed-λ sweep.** λ ∈ {1e-3, 1e-2, 1e-1, 1}, all four arms.
4. **CV-tuned λ.** For each target, seed and arm, λ is chosen by 5-fold CV on that arm's training rows only. Only the held-out exact rows are scored, from the grid {1e-3, 3e-3, 1e-2, 3e-2, 1e-1, 3e-1, 1, 3, 10}.
5. **Independent check.** A separately written implementation (`penalty_sensitivity/independent/`) agrees with every corrected-fit contrast at the four fixed λ to within 0.002.

## How the camera-ready reports this

- The pre-registered verdicts are reported as they were. The tuned results are labelled post hoc wherever they appear.
- For the size-matched contrast, the manuscript now says that bounds read as values "cost a little … always below the floor", and calls it null. A reviewer of the accepted version asked that the direction be stated. The verdict is unchanged: it is null under R4, as in Amendment 4.
- The conclusion is now scoped to regularisation. At the a-priori penalty, deleting bounds costs accuracy. With a tuned penalty it does not. Tobit is worse than naive at every penalty run, but by less than the floor once λ is tuned.
